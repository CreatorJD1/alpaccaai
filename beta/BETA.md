## Rules for contributors

- **Design lock is law.** 01 Companion Mode: cream/ivory oversized hoodie, black shorts,
  right-thigh strap, boots, lanyard/ID badge, long silver-white hair with blue tips,
  hair clip on HER left (viewer's right), bare neck — NO choker. Never improvise outfits.
- **One authoritative CoreMind.** Don't fork her brain; extend it.
- **Honest status.** Every external connection reports its real state (live/dead/unset).
  Never present demo systems as live.
- **Self-reports come from persisted state.** Never invent memories, desires, or availability.
- **Approval-gated.** Risky actions (deletes, purchases, account/credential changes,
  destructive ops) need Jason's approval. No exceptions.
- **Observe → reflect → propose → Jason approves → implement → record.**
- Keep `main`'s dirty work intact — don't commit other people's uncommitted changes.
